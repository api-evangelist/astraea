## Common Questions

- What is Astraea? Astraea is an AI-native clinical trial automation platform for standards-aware biometrics, compliance, and submission workflows.
- What workflows does Astraea automate? Astraea automates and orchestrates SDTM and ADaM mapping, aCRF annotation, Define-XML and eCRT metadata, TLF generation from SAPs, validation checks, data desensitization, evidence synthesis, and audit-ready logs.
- Does Astraea replace clinical experts? No. Astraea runs clinical trial workflows under human supervision; qualified reviewers confirm, correct, or reject critical outputs before acceptance.
- How does Astraea handle regulated records? Astraea is built around 21 CFR Part 11-aligned controls, including audit trails, access controls, validation, record retention, linked electronic signatures, and reviewable lineage.
- How can a sponsor request a demo? Use the contact page or the product-demo scheduling link on Astraea's site.
